Our Story

Kelly's Burger is a small fast-food business concept developed in South Africa in 2026. The idea is to provide tasty and affordable meals for students, working adults, families and people living in the local community.

The business focuses on burgers and cold drinks, while also making space for other food choices and ice-cream treats. The website was created to give customers a convenient place to learn about the business, view the menu and send enquiries without having to search through different sources of information.

As the business grows, the menu and website can be updated with new products, prices and photographs. Customer feedback is also important because it can help improve the products and the service.

Our Mission

To provide fresh, tasty and affordable meals while offering fast, friendly and helpful customer service.

Our Vision

To become a trusted local burger business known for quality food, fair prices and convenient customer service.

Our Values

  • Quality: Provide clear product information and aim for good food quality.
  • Affordability: Offer menu choices at reasonable prices.
  • Customer care: Treat customers with respect and respond to enquiries.
  • Improvement: Use feedback to improve the menu and website.
